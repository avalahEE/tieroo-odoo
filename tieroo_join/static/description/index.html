Customers join with a QR code

A QR poster by the till. Customers scan it, enter their name and email, confirm the email and get their wallet card. In Odoo they become loyalty members, tagged as joined via QR.

Add-on for Tieroo – Loyalty cards

Needs Tieroo – Loyalty cards, on any Tieroo plan including the free one. The join page and the confirmation email run in your Odoo; the details customers enter stay in Odoo.

What it does

  • A join page in your Odoo. Mobile first, in the visitor's language, in your card's colours. No Website app needed.
  • Double opt-in. The customer confirms the email before becoming a member, so the addresses are real.
  • Posters to print. The card designer makes A4 and A5 posters with the QR code (PDF with bleed, or SVG).
  • Your automations. New members get a tag, so Odoo Automation or Marketing Automation can send a welcome email or add them to a list.
  • Protected. Limits per address and per email keep bots and repeated sign-ups out.

Getting started

  1. Install this module next to Tieroo – Loyalty cards.
  2. Settings → Tieroo → Join via QR: choose the tag for new members.
  3. Print the poster from the card designer (QR & poster) and put it by the till.

Requirements

  • Odoo 18, 19 or 20, Community or Enterprise, with Tieroo – Loyalty cards connected.
  • Your Odoo reachable from the internet (the QR code opens a page on it).
