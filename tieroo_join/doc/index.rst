====================
Tieroo – Join via QR
====================

Customers scan a QR code, confirm their email and get their wallet card.

Set up
======

1. Install **Tieroo – Join via QR** next to Tieroo – Loyalty cards.
2. **Settings > Tieroo**: check the *Join link* and choose the tag new members get
   (for example *Joined via QR*).
3. Click **QR code and posters**, print the poster and put it up where customers see it, for example at the checkout.

How it works
============

1. The customer scans the QR code and enters their name and email.
2. Odoo sends a confirmation email. After the customer confirms, they become a loyalty member.
3. The card arrives by email, with *Add to Apple Wallet* and *Add to Google Wallet* buttons.

The join page runs in your Odoo; no Website app is needed. Your Odoo must be reachable from the internet.

Welcome emails and newsletters
==============================

New members get the tag you chose. Use it in Odoo Automation Rules or Marketing Automation,
for example to send a welcome email or add them to a mailing list.
