Loyalty cards in Apple Wallet and Google Wallet

Your Odoo Loyalty cards on your customers' phones, updated after every purchase. Design the card in Odoo, send it by email or with a QR poster, and that's it.

Free up to 100 cards  Odoo 18, 19 and 20 · Community or Enterprise

Tieroo is an external service. The cards are issued and kept up to date by Tieroo (hosted in the EU). Before anything is sent, an administrator ticks a box in Settings to agree; see Data sent to Tieroo below.

How it works

1. Your programme stays in Odoo

Points, rewards and expiry come from Odoo Loyalty. The QR code on the card is the customer's Odoo barcode, so the cashier scans it at the Odoo POS as usual.

2. The card updates itself

After a sale, the card on the phone shows the new points a moment later. Customers also get a lock-screen notice when a reward is ready.

3. Nothing to set up

No Apple or Google developer accounts, no certificates, no keys to copy. Odoo connects to Tieroo by itself when you choose a plan.

The card designer, inside Odoo

Settings → Tieroo → Design the card. Drag the fields onto the places Apple and Google really have, pick the colours, upload the logo and a banner, and see the iPhone and Android card side by side with a sample customer. One save updates every card on every phone.

  • Fields: points, next reward, customer name, company, valid until, programme, rewards list, level.
  • 27 card languages; each customer gets the card in their Odoo language.
  • Checks text contrast and logos that would print blurry.
  • QR posters to print (A4 and A5 PDF with bleed, or SVG).

Cards are sent by email from Odoo, with Add to Apple Wallet and Add to Google Wallet buttons: to every member with an email when you switch it on, then to each new member. A customer who lost the email gets it again with one click.

Getting started

  1. Install Tieroo – Loyalty cards. Odoo Loyalty and Point of Sale come with it.
  2. Fill in your company's VAT number and country (Settings → Companies).
  3. Settings → Tieroo: tick the box to agree to send data, then Start with Tieroo. The plan page opens with your company's details filled in.
  4. Choose a plan and confirm with Stripe. A payment card is asked for the free plan too, but nothing is charged.
  5. Back in Odoo, you are connected. Choose the loyalty programme, design the card and send it.

Plans

Free up to 100 cards. Bigger plans are priced by cards in use, monthly or yearly, and are shown on the plan page that opens from Settings → Tieroo. Change or cancel at any time.

Data sent to Tieroo

Nothing is sent until an administrator agrees in Settings → Tieroo. Then Odoo sends:

  • To open the account: the company's name, VAT number and address, and the administrator's email.
  • To issue and update the cards: each card holder's name, barcode (the QR code), points, next reward, level, card expiry and language, and their company for business customers.
  • Card holders' email addresses are not sent: the emails with the cards come from your Odoo.

Your loyalty data stays in Odoo, which remains the master. Privacy policy: https://tieroo.com/privacy.html · Terms: https://tieroo.com/terms.html

Requirements

  • Odoo 18, 19 or 20, Community or Enterprise, on Odoo.sh or your own server. Odoo Online cannot install third-party apps.
  • The Python package requests (included in standard Odoo installations).
  • Outgoing email that reaches your customers: the cards go out by email from Odoo. With a company email on your own domain, connect Odoo to that domain's mail server (Settings → General Settings → Emails → Custom Email Servers), or the emails may land in spam.

Add-ons

  • Tieroo – Customer Levels: spend-based levels such as Silver and Gold, each with its own Odoo pricelist, shown on the card.
  • Tieroo – Join via QR: customers scan a QR code, confirm their email and get the card. No Website app needed.
