========================
Tieroo – Customer Levels
========================

Spend-based levels such as Silver and Gold, each with its own Odoo pricelist, shown on the wallet card.

Set up
======

1. Install **Tieroo – Customer Levels** next to Tieroo – Loyalty cards.
2. **Settings > Tieroo > Plan and billing**: add Customer Levels to your plan.
3. **Point of Sale > Configuration > Customer Levels**: create the levels, the spend for each
   and the pricelist it gives. Separate levels for private customers (B2C) and companies (B2B).
4. In the card designer, give each level its own banner if you like.

How customers move
==================

* Each customer has a personal 12-month period.
* A customer moves up as soon as an order is paid or an invoice is posted.
* At the end of the period, that period's spend sets the level, up or down.
* A company's spend includes all its contact persons; they all get the company's level and pricelist.

The card shows the level and what keeps it, for example *€120 more by 31 Dec*.
On iPhone the customer gets a notice when the level changes.
