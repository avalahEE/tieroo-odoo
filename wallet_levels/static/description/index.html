Customer Levels on the wallet card

Spend-based levels such as Silver and Gold. Each level gives its own Odoo pricelist, and the wallet card shows the level and what keeps it.

Add-on for Tieroo – Loyalty cards

Needs Tieroo – Loyalty cards and a paid Tieroo plan with the Customer Levels add-on, added on the plan page. Tieroo is an external service; see the Loyalty cards app for the data it receives.

What it does

  • Levels from spend. Each customer has a personal 12-month period. A customer moves up as soon as an order is paid or an invoice posted; at the end of the period, that period's spend sets the level, up or down.
  • A pricelist per level. Gold customers get the Gold pricelist in the POS and in sales.
  • B2C and B2B. Levels per contact, or per company, so everyone buying for that company gets its pricelist.
  • On the card. The wallet card shows the level and what keeps it, for example "€120 more by 31 Dec", and each level can have its own banner.
  • Notices. On iPhone the customer gets a notice when the level changes; on Android the card changes quietly.

Getting started

  1. Install this module next to Tieroo – Loyalty cards.
  2. Settings → Tieroo → Plan and billing: add Customer Levels to your plan.
  3. Point of Sale → Configuration → Customer Levels: create the levels, their spend and pricelists.
  4. Design the level banners in the card designer.

Requirements

  • Odoo 18, 19 or 20, Community or Enterprise, with Tieroo – Loyalty cards connected.
